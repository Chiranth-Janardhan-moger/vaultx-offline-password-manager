VaultX is a fully offline, privacy-first password and identity manager that keeps your credentials secure on your device. Your data never leaves your phone and never touches the cloud.

KEY FEATURES:
• 100% Offline - Zero cloud dependencies, no remote servers
• AES-256 Encryption - Robust CBC mode encryption with PBKDF2 key derivation
• Biometric Unlock - Fingerprint and Face Unlock support
• Identity Wallet - Store Aadhaar, PAN, voter IDs, credit and debit cards securely
• Built-in 2FA Authenticator - Time-based one-time password (TOTP) generator
• Smart Categories - Automatically organizes credentials by service type
• Password Generator - Standalone, cryptographically secure password generator
• Encrypted Backup & Restore - Password-protected backup export and import
• Auto-lock Timer - Configurable inactivity timeout
• Custom Folders & Favorites - Flexible organization for quick access
• PIN Protection - 6-digit PIN fallback unlock method

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
• Zero external tracking or analytics
• No cloud storage or syncing
• Open source and auditable architecture
• Encrypted local storage with salted key derivation
• Hardware-backed FLAG_SECURE screenshot and screen-recording protection

VaultX is designed for users who prioritize privacy and want total sovereignty over their credentials. All encryption and decryption operations happen strictly on-device.

Minimal permissions required:
• Biometric hardware for secure unlock
• Vibration for feedback
• Storage access (Android 12 and below, for backup export and import)

No internet or overlay permissions required.
